Valerie Kirsten is a Senior Project Manager at RAL, steering projects through every phase of design and construction. She began her career in interior design, architecture and construction on projects ranging from commercial interiors and healthcare complexes to multifamily residential and luxury hospitality.

That breadth helps her communicate well with every member of a project team, and she combines her design background, technical knowledge and people skills to manage projects from planning through completion. Valerie holds a Bachelor of Arts in Architecture from Princeton University and is a licensed architect in Colorado.
