Josh Wein is responsible for property acquisitions, site valuation, financial modeling and the management of project budgets, and he leads RAL's digital and marketing efforts. Since joining RAL in 2006, he has managed the underwriting, financing, rezoning and approvals process for more than five million square feet of development across the United States and the Caribbean.

He is involved in the business and financing structure of ongoing developments and works with partners, lenders, elected officials and community members on new and existing properties. Josh holds a BS in Information Technology and Management with a minor in Entrepreneurship and Emerging Enterprises, a professional certificate in Real Estate Finance & Investments from New York University, and is a New York State licensed real estate broker.
