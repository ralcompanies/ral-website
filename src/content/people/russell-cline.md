Russell Cline is an Associate Project Manager at RAL, supporting the delivery of luxury hospitality and residential developments in the Colorado mountains. With a focus on budgeting, scheduling and project coordination, he helps guide complex projects from planning through construction.

Russell is driven by turning ambitious development visions into thoughtfully built places that people enjoy. He holds a bachelor's degree in Real Estate Finance and Real Property Development from California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo.
