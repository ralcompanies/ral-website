Robert A. Levine is the founder and CEO of RAL. Since founding the firm in 1979, he has grown it to include five affiliate companies and a skilled staff that coordinates and oversees every part of developing luxury residential, resort and commercial properties, from financing and design to construction and property management.

A developer trained as a planner and architect, Robert has been responsible for more than 100 luxury residential, hotel and mixed-use projects across the country, with total project values in the billions of dollars.
