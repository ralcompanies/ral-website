Robert A. Levine founded RAL in 1979 and has grown it into a group of affiliated companies with a skilled professional staff. RAL coordinates every facet of developing luxury residential, resort and commercial properties, from financing and design to construction and property management.

Trained as a planner and architect, Mr. Levine has been responsible for the development of more than 100 luxury residential, hotel and mixed-use projects across the country.
