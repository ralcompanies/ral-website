Jerry Gallo is Director of Construction at RAL, where he oversees construction and serves as a lead owner's representative across the firm's development portfolio. He manages project teams, coordinates with owners, consultants and contractors, and guides projects from preconstruction through completion.

Jerry brings experience in luxury residential renovations, ground-up construction, hospitality and mixed-use development throughout New York. He earned his Bachelor of Architecture from the New York Institute of Technology and pairs a design background with extensive construction management expertise.
