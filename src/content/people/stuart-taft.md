Stuart Taft manages RAL's acquisition, leasing and finance teams. Since joining RAL in 2001 he has been responsible for the acquisition and financing of developments totaling more than six million square feet and $2.5 billion: luxury condominium towers and affordable rental housing, technology offices and ski and beach resorts, ground-up development and conversions, in gateway U.S. cities and on the Caribbean coast.

He oversees the review, structuring and execution of RAL's real estate and financial transactions, including acquisitions, financing, dispositions, capitalization, joint ventures and development agreements, and all office and retail leasing in the portfolio. His more than 30 years in real estate include roles as Director of Real Estate Development at Globix Corporation, Vice President of Real Estate Portfolio Management at The Chase Manhattan Bank, Retail Property Manager for The Rouse Company at South Street Seaport, and Real Estate Associate in the investment and merchant banking group of The Tribeca Corporation. He holds a master's degree in Real Estate Investment and Development from New York University and a BA from the State University of New York at Albany, and is a licensed New York real estate broker.
