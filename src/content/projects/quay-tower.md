    body: >-
      ODA designed a metal-and-glass tower; Marmol Radziner, in its first East Coast project,
      wrapped the residences in California-modernist interiors. RAL planned combination units from
      the outset, so larger homes could be created by opening a single wall. West-facing residences
      have private elevators, and residents share two indoor-outdoor roof lounges.
